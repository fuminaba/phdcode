How phdcode works

phdcode keeps your literature and gives tools to a chat. The chat (the harness and its model) is outside phdcode: Claude Code or dcode. MCP connects the two. Some parts run with no chat: the daily sweep, the summaries and the GPU rules.

2. A RAG question

sequenceDiagram participant You participant H as Harness (dcode or Claude Code) participant P as phdcode-mcp participant DB as Postgres You->>H: a question H->>P: search_papers(query, facet) P->>DB: hybrid search: meaning (vectors) + keywords, fused by rank DB-->>P: the best papers P-->>H: titles, abstracts or summaries, scores H->>P: search_chunks(query, paper_ids) P->>DB: the same hybrid search over the passages P-->>H: passages, each with its pages H-->>You: the answer: title, page and an exact quote for each claim

The facet selects what search_papers searches: concept (title and abstract, all papers), methods or results (the summaries, full-text papers only).

The tools that a chat can call

ToolWhat it doesWrites?
search_sourcesSearches arXiv, PubMed or OpenAlex live.no
get_paperThe details of one paper, at the source or stored.no
search_papersHybrid search over the stored papers, by facet.no
search_chunksHybrid search over the full text: passages with pages.no
list_saved_searchesThe active saved searches.no
save_paperStores an abstract-only paper.yes
ingest_pdfMakes a full-text paper from a reference or a local PDF.yes
add_saved_searchAdds a standing query with its interest statement and threshold.yes
cancel_saved_searchStops a saved search.yes

A tool that writes needs your approval in the dcode TUI. Headless dcode (dcode -n) can run only the tools that do not write.
